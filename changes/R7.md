## Changelog lines (user-facing, for [Unreleased] → Changed / Added / Fixed / Removed)

Changed
- Notes is redrawn: the tree on the left with Pinned, Recent and your folders, the note in a white box, and beside it what the note is about, starting with its relations in words.
- With no note open, Notes shows Notes home: recent notes, pinned notes, your inbox, your counts (notes, conversations, open and done tasks) and your life areas. Notes opens on the note you had open last until you close it.
- The editor reads like the boards: bold and italic without their stars, bullets for list dashes, due dates as "today" or "3 days late", wide tables scroll sideways.
- Make this a project is offered on ordinary notes only, in the note's ··· menu.
- The document reader slides in from the right like the other sheets.

Added
- Pin a note to Notes home from its ··· menu; the pin is a `pinned: true` line in the note's own front matter, so it shows on every device and in Obsidian.
- Show in the Orrery on every note.
- A tag in the side panel filters the tree to the notes carrying it.

Fixed
- Typing is no longer lost when a save fails (for instance while Orrama restarts after an update): it is retried on its own, kept if you move to another note, kept on the device if the page closes, and offered back the next time you open the note. Reloading sends what you just typed.
- A note renamed or deleted in Obsidian while open no longer comes back as a duplicate under its old name when you type.
- Cmd-click on a link with an alias (`[[Note|label]]`) or a path opens the right note.
- Find can always reopen a note, also the one you arrived on after you moved on or closed it.
- Saving no longer reloads the whole note list twice.

Removed
- The map's "Show" category select and its five chips (one life-area menu instead).

## Board vs app: differences left on purpose

- The side panel shows Tasks (the board leaves them out because they sit in the note): structure.md §2.3 lists them second, and wave B's task fixes live there.
- On the phone the side panel shows all its sections below the note, not only Relations (the board crops it): nothing is lost on a phone.
- On a typed vault the Relations box also says what the note "mentions" (its links the graph doesn't already tell), as the board does; on a vault without an ontology it says "mentions" and "mentioned in".
- Due dates sit right after the task text, not in a right-aligned column: CodeMirror lines wrap, and a right column would be a block widget per task.
- The phone's tree opens from a button in the note's top row, not from the top bar's title (the shell is R0's).
- Notes home has no board; it is built from the system's modules on the Notes board's language.
- No "Suggested links" popover in the editor ("Link to Emma Jansen?"): no endpoint knows unlinked mentions. Not built.
- "Long re-index: Carry, with a real progress bar" and "Loading the index: Reading 312 notes…": the server reports no index progress. The tree shows grey rows while loading.
- The ontology note's link to Orr noticed is R11's (the page doesn't exist yet).
