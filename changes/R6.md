## Board vs app: differences left on purpose

- **Events have their own lane, "Calendar".** The board puts events and lectures in area
  lanes (Kade standup in Work, Supervision in School), but an event is a row in the calendar
  note with no note behind it, so the app can't know its area. Blocks and due steps are in
  their note's area. If Jules wants events in areas, a `| Area |` column in the calendar note
  (or matching titles against `#vault-categories`) is a server change: ask.
- **"+ Add to Saturday" adds the step to the inbox**, due that day, whatever lane it was
  clicked in: there is no endpoint that creates a step in an area's note. The dialog says so.
- **Lane notes** say what the week holds ("Graduation thesis · 2 late", "3 projects"), not the
  board's prose ("review Friday", "Half marathon in 10 days").
- **Orr on the horizon is Hello at 36 px** (R0's Orr is Hello below 48 px; the board's
  sprite reads); Sit when the week is open.
- **The task card is a dialog-kind layer**, not a sheet, so it opens over the To plan sheet
  instead of replacing it (one of a kind per layer); it floats beside its item as on the board.
- **To plan's cuts** are the board's Late / This week / All; the old At risk cut is gone,
  shown instead as "at risk" on the step's line.
- **The per-day load bars** (under each column head and in month cells) are gone: the boards
  show booked hours as a number only.
- **The estimates footnote** ("Estimates are heuristic; a day holds 6h…") sits under To plan
  beside the hours; the boards don't draw it.
- **"Booked … 1.5h" lengths**: the server snaps blocks to 30 minutes, so a typed "1h45" books
  2h, as before.
- **The hours' inner scrollbar is hidden** (WebKit with a mouse narrowed the columns under the
  day heads otherwise); wheel, trackpad and keys scroll it.
- Long month and weekday names ("5–11 October", "Thursday") are composed in
  `calendarMath.ts`; `ui/dates.ts` has only the short forms. Candidates to promote.
- Plan my week is on Week and Day, not Month (the month board has none).
