## Board vs app: differences left on purpose
- **Last in touch** is the newest note that links to the person (its `date:` or when it was saved) and its title, not mail or meetings: the app has no per-person mail history. Jules chose this on 11 Oct. Contact, company, project and ontology notes don't count; a note dated in the future doesn't either.
- **"All areas" is "All sectors"** (Jules's choice, 11 Oct): life areas would put every contact in one bucket.
- **The phone's New (+)** sits beside the search, not in the top bar: the shell's top bar has no page action slot yet (the Today board has the same +; R5). A filter button beside it opens the sector menu, which the phone board doesn't show.
- **The top bar and window title of a person** read from the slug ("Ines vermaat"): App's `titleOf` has no names. Fix in `titleOf` or give pages a way to name themselves.
- **Relations on the detail Sheet** are not shown: the sheet is today's card until R11's entity pages.
- **Companies' A–Z runs**: the board shows none for 18 companies; the app groups people only.
- Rows are `role="button"` (not `<a>`), because the names inside them are links of their own (§1.7 #1); a nested link inside an `<a>` is invalid. Cmd-click on a row doesn't open a new tab; cmd-click on a name does.
