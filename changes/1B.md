## Decided here (brief said "decide")
- Priority words: None / High / Medium / Low for none / `!p1` / `!p2` / `!p3`, a quiet segmented choice, never a colour (the thesis marks nearly every step `!p1`).
- The editor's box: a click does what the To-do's box does (open, in progress, blocked → done; done → open). In progress and blocked come from the card or the To-do's menu, never by cycling the box.
- Escape while editing the words closes the card, unsaved, as Escape does in every dialog (the overlay stack takes Escape on window capture).
- Delete leaves a promoted step's booked rows in the calendar note (unshown, the object orphaned) as any deleted line does today; it does not delete calendar rows.
