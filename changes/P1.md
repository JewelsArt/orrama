# P1: Planning opens at once (the AI estimate stops blocking)

## Built
- `GET /api/plan` answers at once: the model's estimate where the cache has one, the heuristic otherwise, with `refining` per task and on the plan; the model is asked after the response, one call at a time, one batch (≤ 200 tasks) per call (commit 255743e)
- Estimates cached per task, keyed on (model, the task's text, priority, project): one changed task is one call for that task, a tick asks nothing; a failed call leaves those tasks to the heuristic for 10 minutes instead of retrying on every load (255743e)
- `GET /api/plan?refined=1`: waits for the model (bounded by twice `ORRAMA_PLAN_AI_TIMEOUT_MS`) and returns the refined plan; response schema + contract row (255743e)
- Planning asks `?refined=1` once when the plan says `refining`, swaps the plan in quietly, holds it while a pointer is down or a step is dragged, and says "heuristic until AI refines them" meanwhile (0481add)

## Measured
Scratch vault, 113 → 104 open tasks (one ticked between loads, as a working day does), a fake OpenAI-compatible model that answers in 6 s, 10 loads of `GET /api/plan`:

| | average | max | model calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| before | 6,025 ms | 6,071 ms | 10 (one per load, the whole list each time) |
| after | 11 ms | 32 ms | 1 (the first load's 112 unknown tasks); the refined plan 6 s after the first load |
