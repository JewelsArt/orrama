## CLAUDE.md changes to apply (exact text, and where)
- In "## The Finance area", replace the paragraph that starts "`loadTxns` (reached through `txnsFor`" with:

  > `loadStatements` (reached through `statementsFor`, which applies the reader's own-accounts list; `txnsFor` is its rows alone) and `buildRules` are shared by all four endpoints and the Dashboard on purpose: three copies of a de-duplication rule is three chances for two pages to disagree about what a month cost. **One quantity, one function**: what a month cost is `costOf` through `monthlyByCategory` (the hero, the month page, the category page and the Dashboard all read it); the savings plan is `planSavings`; "this month against typical" is `typicalAround` (the complete months around it, itself excluded — the month page, the top cards and the Dashboard card); whether a ledger line matches a description is `matchesTerms`; in-minus-out of the ledgers is `netTotals`, per currency, never summed across currencies. When a month view is filtered to a category, its "typical" must be that category's median, not the whole month's — comparing €250 of shopping against a €1,000 all-category median is the bug the finance tests exist to catch.

- In "## The Finance area", append to the paragraph "**A statement is read in one currency and one pocket, in time order.**":

  > The main pocket is `mainPocket` in `statement.ts`, and everything that reads a balance — the Balance card, the trend behind burn and runway, the savings plan's outflow and own-account savings — reads that pocket only. **Complete months come from coverage, not from spending**: each export covers its first row to its last, two exports join when they overlap, touch, or the running balance carries across the gap (`coverageOf`), and a month is complete only when covered from its first day to its last (`fullMonths`). A covered month with nothing spent is a €0 month; the drift is measured per contiguous run of complete months.

## Not fixed (and why) / needs Jules
- REG-003, partly by nature: the acceptance's "a runway computed from it" cannot happen today — runway needs a burn, burn needs the statement's balance trend, and a statement with balances supplies its own balance, which wins over the typed one. `balance: 1.234` (no comma) is already a YAML number and stays 1.234.
- FIN-014, left alone on purpose: the All-spending page's category rows still set last month against the category's overall typical shown in the same row (they open the category page, not the month page). Seen in the browser: Shopping reads "▼ €9 under usual" there and "▼ €11" on the top card. Say if those rows should use `lastTypical` too.
- FIN-008 trade-off: a Revolut CSV does not record the period it was exported for, so a month whose first or last day has no transaction (and no other file or carried balance covering it) is treated as not covered — one month lost, never a half month counted. Existing fixtures that started on the 5th/10th while meaning a complete first month now start on the 1st.
- Other instances of root cause #7 seen outside my files (for S08 — ledgers, cadences): the ledger page's `dueIn` adds item amounts across currencies ("−€31.66" for €19.66 + $12), and each item's "/mo" figure and calendar chip print with `eur()` whatever the item's currency (GitHub $12 shows as −€12.00).

- Test environment: the watcher tests (calendar-notes, duplicate-ids, vanishing-notes, new-folder-watch, editor-sync, crash-loops) failed intermittently during this session at load average 20–30 and failed the same way on the untouched base `53f09aa`; the Vite dev server also missed a file change once.
