## Board vs app: differences left on purpose
- **Entity, "Ines has no phone number. Add one?"**: no graph rule says a person needs a phone; the warning box shows only the graph's own warnings. Nothing is invented.
- **Entity, "Unanswered" mail and Reply at a row's end**: the app knows unread, not unanswered; the row opens the message, and a Reply link inside a row link would be a control inside a control (WEB-018).
- **Entity, "All →" on With Ines**: no list of everything with one person exists to open; left out.
- **Entity, "mentioned in … + 4 more"**: the same, with "+ N more" opening the rest in place.
- **Entity, "How you know Camille Roux" on Ines's page**: the board's example names someone else; the page says how you know *this* person, from your own person note, and only for a short indirect path. No "you" note → no block.
- **Entity, phone**: the spec's order (warnings, facts, relations, activity, then the note) rather than the board's (note before activity); the top bar says the name, so the page's h1 hides, as on the project page.
- **Entity, a document's Open**: the reader is in the page (DocViewer's inline frame), so a second full-screen Open was dropped; Download stays in the reader's head.
- **Find, groups**: "Notes and conversations" is one group as on the board (§2.12 lists them apart). Keycaps show inside Find only, and say "Enter" in words: the one-hand rule keeps ↵ off screen.
- **Find, "employs you and 6 more"**: said as "N people you know"; "you" needs your own person note, which most vaults won't have.
- **Find, documents**: notes the ontology types `document` come through the note search as notes; Find does not ask `/api/graph/entities`.
- **Orr noticed, "Use at risk"** (states board): keeping the field's word means removing a tag, which no endpoint does safely; the second button is Open the note. "Not a company" (states board) is not offered.
- **Orr noticed**: no board draws the whole page; it is built from §2.15 and the states board's warning cards.
