# R8: Projects and the project page

Overview became **Projects**, and the project page became the richest entity page: header,
then Overview, Timeline, Documents and To-do at their own addresses. Its shared pieces are in
`apps/web/src/entity/` for R11. Branch `design/R8-projects`, 5 commits on `29aab8f` (wave B).

## Built
- The entity page's shared pieces: header, status vocabulary and menu, dates as typed text, relation rows, Orr's warnings (e2b6d5a)
- On a phone, a task checkbox stays round: `button.cb` opts out of the shell's 44 px rule (148b5a9)
- The project page: the header, four tabs at `/projects/<slug>[/timeline|/documents|/todo]`, the Overview as one scroll, Timeline with the Gantt under it, To-do by the note's sections, the Documents board with Orr's suggested links one at a time, Add a step (198520f)

## Changelog lines (user-facing, for [Unreleased] → Changed / Added / Fixed / Removed)

Changed
- Overview is now **Projects**: one row per project with its next step, status, next date, key deadline and people; filter by life area (the five areas); paused, cancelled, completed and archived projects sit on shelves under the board.
- A project's status uses the vault's seven words (planned, on track, at risk, paused, completed, archived, cancelled). Older words in a note (sleeping, planning, done, blocked…) are read as the word they mean.
- The project page has a new header: status as a menu, dates as text you can type ("19 Jan 2027"), the days left, the area and the steps, Add a step, and a ⋯ menu with Show in the Orrery.
- The project's Overview is one scroll: the note on the left; decisions, inbox, next steps, goals, people, conversations, documents and the prompt on the right.
- The Gantt moved from the Projects board into each project's Timeline ("Show the Gantt").
- Orr's suggested links on a project's Documents board are asked about one at a time: Link or No.

Added
- Each project tab has its own address (`/projects/graduation-thesis/timeline`), and the board's area filter too (`/projects?area=school`).
- Add a step, from the project's header, its To-do tab or an empty week on its Timeline, with a typed due date.
- People & companies on a project shows who supervises it, from the knowledge graph, and the project's conversations.
- Orr points out a project with no status, or a status outside the vault's words; when every step is done he offers to archive it.

Fixed
- A project's next date on the board is its next open step's, not the earliest date of any step, done or not.
- On a phone, task checkboxes were stretched into ovals.
- Dates no longer show as 08/31/2026 in the desktop app.

Removed
- The board's per-project Gantt switch and its Sleep, Wake and Cancel buttons (now the ⋯ menu on a row and on the project page).
- The P1 chip on a project's steps.

## The entity page's pieces, for R11 (`apps/web/src/entity/`, styles in `styles/entity.css`)

| Piece | Use |
|---|---|
| `EntityHeader({ crumbs, lead, title, meta, actions })` | The header. `lead` is 44 px (36 on the phone): a `ProgressRing`, `Ring`, `Square` or a type badge. `meta` is a row of items that wrap; put `.ent-link` on any that opens something. Actions with `className="ent-wide"` hide on the phone. On the project page the last crumb is visually hidden (the title says it) and the h1 hides on the phone (the top bar says it): both are `.pj-page` rules, so decide per page. |
| `StatusMenu({ value, canEdit, onPick, capital })`, `statusLook(raw)` | Status as a dot and a word; a menu of the seven when editable. `onPick` gets the ontology id. Unknown words show as written, empty as "no status" with a hollow dot. |
| `statusOf`, `shelfOf`, `statusProblems`, `STATUS_IDS`, `STATUS_WORD` | The vocabulary as data. `statusProblems` gives Orr's line for a missing or unknown status; drop it when the graph already warns about status (see `Project.tsx`). |
| `DatesButton({ slots, canEdit, onCommit })`, `rangeLabel`, `parseDateInput` | Two dates as "31 Aug – 19 Jan"; a popover of typed fields. `onCommit(key, iso | "")` is called once per field left; serialise the writes yourself (`Project.tsx`'s `patch` queue). |
| `useRelations(noteId, refresh)` | `/api/graph/relations` for one note: `by` (relation id → notes) and `warnings`. Empty without an ontology, never an error. |
| `rolesOf(by)`, `roleWord(rel)` | The word for each related note from the entity's side (`supervised_by` → "supervises"), strongest first. Add rows to `ROLE` for a person's or company's relations (`works_at`, `employs` are there). |
| `RelationRows({ items })` | Rows: ring (person), square (company), chat or note icon, title, meta, the word at the end; `onOpen` or `href`. |
| `OrrWarnings({ warnings, actions })` | The `oprop boxed` box with Orr's head above the page; nothing when there are none. |

Not built here and needed by R11: facts (front matter) as a block, "How you know them" (`find_path`), activity, a conversation's transcript as turns. `ProjectOverview.tsx`'s `PersonSheet` is a stopgap for a person opened from a project; once the person page exists, a person row should go to `/people/<slug>`.

## Board vs app: differences left on purpose
- **Next date's second line** ("2h left", "15:00", "until 12:00"): the board draws times from booked blocks; the board's data has only due dates. Shown: "was <date>" under a late step, nothing otherwise.
- **Timeline**: no "booked time" cards and no "Plan the next weeks" / Orr's hatched proposals: steps carry no time blocks in the workspace, and nothing proposes step dates yet. The legend shows only what can appear (due, key deadline). The "now" highlight is the week, not an in-progress card.
- **Conversations' meta** ("Tue 6 Oct · with Claude"): the graph's relations carry no date; rows show the title only. The module hides when there are none.
- **Phone header**: the board keeps the big title in the page with "‹ Projects" in the top bar; the shell's top bar shows the page's title instead, so the page hides its own h1 on the phone rather than say it twice. The board's top-bar ⋯ is the header's ⋯ (still on screen).
- **Phone board's +** (New project in the top bar) is the shell's; the page keeps its own New project button on the phone instead.
- **Overview tab's "Next steps" and "Milestones"** are text in the board's note; here Next steps is a module on the right (structure.md §2.5 item 2), and the note shows whatever the note says.
- **Goals** keep their module (with Edit, COR-005) although the board does not draw one: the behaviour is S05's, and the note on the left shows the same section.
- **Documents board** boxes are drawn uncoloured as on the board; their JSON Canvas colour stays in the file. "Link two" (making a link by hand) is kept though the board has no button for it.
- **Today's summary** (Orr's digest of the board, only with a model set up) is kept as a quiet Orr button in the header; no board draws it.
- **Key deadline** falls back to the note's `deadline:` ("Deadline · Mon 19 Jan") when no open step carries `#deadline`.
- The P1 chip on steps is gone (the thesis marks nearly every step `!p1`; Planning keeps priority).
