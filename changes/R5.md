## Board vs app: differences left on purpose

- **Money's "on pace … by now"**: the board says "On pace with a typical October (€964 by now)". The server knows only a typical whole month and spending isn't even across a month (rent on the 1st), so Today says "A typical month comes to €1,353; 78% of that is spent so far." and the bar has no "by now" marker. Jules, 11 Oct: keep it that way. A real "by day N" typical is a server figure for R10 or a follow-up.
- **Due's Orr proposal** ("Both thesis tasks wait on Thijs Brouwer, and you meet tomorrow…"): the graph doesn't type tasks, so nothing says a task waits on a person. The slot (`.oprop` in Due) is not drawn; nothing is invented.
- **Orr noticed's first warning in words**: `/api/graph/schema` gives warning codes and counts, not the notes, so it says "3 people have no company, and 1 more." rather than "Pieter Bakker works at Bakker & Zn…". R11's Orr noticed page can add the per-note list; then this module can name the first.
- **"2h left"** (remaining estimate) on Today's rows: not shown; the estimate comes from `/api/plan`, which Today asks only when a task card opens. Booked rows say "until 12:00" or the start time.
- **Mail "to answer"**: the board's count is "2 to answer"; the app knows unread, not "needs an answer", so it says "1 unread" (Primary only).
- **People today on the board** matches "Lunch with Emma" to Emma Jansen by first name; Today needs the whole name (or the address on today's mail), so nobody is matched on a guess.
- **Phone**: the board hides People today, the third column and the foot; Today stacks all seven in §2.1's order (Due, Mail, People today, Coming up, Money, Projects, Orr noticed) and keeps the foot, so Customise is reachable. The third late row and "Later this week" are hidden on a phone, as on the board.
- **New**: Event and Project go to Planning's day and the Projects board (their create flows are R6's and R8's); Note opens the name dialog; Task opens Capture. Subscription and Income stay here (Jules, 11 Oct) until Money has its own.
- **Empty timetable slots** don't create a block yet (§1.7 #4): creating time is Planning's (R6).
- **Overlapping items** on the track draw over each other on one row, as the board has room for one.
