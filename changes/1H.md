## Built
- Server: the calendar note's table gains **Place | Notes | Repeat** after the id, added only when
  the first row needs them (an old note reads, and is rewritten around a new plain row, byte for
  byte). `all day` in Start is an all-day event. Repeat is typed words (`weekly`, `every 2 weeks`,
  `monthly`, `yearly`, `until <day>`, `skip <days>`; `parseRepeat`/`repeatCell`). A series is
  expanded by the ICS reader (`ownVEvent` + `expandIcsReport`), so it lands on the days a subscribed
  calendar with the same rule does. `ownEvents()` is the one reader of your events: `GET
  /api/events`, Today's agenda, Plan my week's busy hours. `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/events/<id>@<day>`
  with `scope` one|all; `eventWriteSchema`. `PATCH /api/calendar/feeds/:id` (`feedPatchSchema`)
  renames a feed and gives it a colour `c1`–`c5`; occurrences carry `feedId` and `color`. The
  published feed takes every block in your own calendar notes (not only steps naming you), and
  sends a series as one VEVENT with RRULE/EXDATE, LOCATION, DESCRIPTION. Schema 5. (1b8a619)
- Web: one event form (`planning/EventForm.tsx`): What, Day, Starts, Ends, All day, Place, Notes,
  Repeats + Until. Used by New → Event, a double-click in the hours, and Edit in an event's
  popover. Your event's popover reads like a subscribed one (when, how it repeats, place as a map
  link, notes with links), with Edit and Delete; a day of a series saves and deletes as "This one" /
  "All" ("Save this one" goes when the repeat itself changed). Booked time's popover takes a typed
  day, start and end (Save) instead of the length buttons. A subscribed calendar drawn in its colour
  (`.tm.fd.tinted`). Plan my week on the phone. Settings → Connections: ⋯ on a calendar → Rename…,
  No colour, Teal, Orange, Blue, Coral, Sky blue (the five area colours). Popovers stay inside the
  window when they grow. (commit 2)

## Rows left, and why
- An event spanning several days (all-day over a week, past midnight): one day only, as before.
- Repeat rules beyond the four (every weekday, the 2nd Tuesday): a hand-typed RRULE word the parser
  doesn't know leaves the row a one-off; the four cover what the brief asked.
- A "this one" day becomes an event of its own: a later "All" change doesn't reach it (as Google's
  "only this event" detaches it). Said here, not on screen.
