## Board vs app: differences left on purpose
- The board (`projects.html`) draws Paused, Cancelled and **Completed**; the app's third shelf is **Done** (Jules's call), holding archived work too.
- "Newest first" has no completion date to go by: no endpoint says when a project was finished. Done sorts by `lastActivityMs` (the latest mtime of the project's notes that hold its steps). Editing an old project's note moves it up; a project with no steps has no such time and sits last, by name.
