### The width

`styles/shell.css` sets three tokens on `.app`: `--page-content: 1440px`, `--page-gutter: 40px`
and `--page-max` (their sum, 1520). One rule, `.main > * { max-width: var(--page-max);
margin-inline: auto }`, caps whatever page is showing and centres it in the space beside the
rail. Ask Orr's panel pushes the frame, so the page centres in what's left. Pages with a gutter
pad by `--page-gutter`: Today, Planning, Projects, a project, People, entity pages, Money, Orr
noticed, and Mail when it isn't connected. Notes, Mail, the Orrery and Settings fill the same box
with their own columns. Phone padding is unchanged.

### The dark button

`ThemeButton` in `ui/shell/Shell.tsx`, at the rail's foot above Find. While the page is light it
shows the moon and "Dark"; while it's dark, the sun and "Light". The tooltip and `aria-label` say
what a click does ("Switch to dark"). A click calls App's `changeTheme("light" | "dark")`, which
saves the choice to the account and turns off Match this device, so the click picks the opposite
of what's showing. The circular wipe grows out of the button. It has no key and no keycap. On the
phone, the theme stays in Settings (More → Settings).

**structure.md §8 #4:** Dark is back in the rail (Jules, 13 October); Reload and Back stay out.
The row in structure.md already says so; R0's notes and the shell's comments said "Reload, Dark
and Back left the rail", and the comments are updated.

### Names

`isIdTitle(title, noteId?)` treats a title as an id when it is either:
- the note's own id (`n-…`), or
- dash-separated hex groups, at least eight hex characters, containing at least one digit and
  one letter.

So "394a1725", "c0ffee12-bead" and a UUID are ids. "facade", "decade", "cafe-babe", "2026-10"
and "20261014" are not.

`displayTitle` turns an id title into the kind, the date and the start of the gist:
"Conversation, 4 Jun 2026: Tidying the reading list for the literature review and…".
- The kind is Conversation or Note.
- The date comes from `date:`, else `created:`, else the file's day.
- The gist is `describeNote`'s, cut at a word, at most 60 characters, with no full stop of its
  own (a name sits inside sentences).

`VaultIndex.upsert` sets a note's `title` to that name and keeps the written title as
`writtenTitle`. Links (`[[394a1725]]`) still resolve by the written title, and search still ranks
by it. The note and its file are never touched.

Every surface reads its names from the index: the tree, search, backlinks, the graph and its
warnings, Orr noticed, Today, Find, the Orrery, entity pages and relation lines. Checked on a
scratch vault with two id-titled session notes and a note titled "facade". Find still finds the
note when you type the id.
