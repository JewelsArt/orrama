## Changelog lines (user-facing, for [Unreleased] → Fixed / Security / Internal)
Fixed:
- Hand-typed amounts in the ledgers read as written: `€1.200` is twelve hundred (it was 1.2), `€29.99 (was 24.99)` is 29.99 (it was 299,924.99), and `−12.00`, `– 12,00` and `12.00-` are negative. A cell holding two numbers is not guessed.
- Ledger cadences beyond the four: biweekly/fortnightly, 4-weekly (vierwekelijks), every 2 months, half-yearly/per semester, and "every N weeks/months". A cadence or amount the ledger cannot read is listed on the Commitments/Income page ("2 rows are not counted") instead of being counted as monthly.
- Rent due on the 31st stays on the 31st in Planning and on the Finance ledger calendar (it drifted to the 30th, then the 28th), and Planning shows the charges before a ledger line's next date.
- `.xlsx` statements and CSVs re-saved by Excel (semicolons, decimal commas, day-month-year dates) are read. Uploading a statement says how many transactions it added, or why it adds none; statement files left out of the figures are listed on the Finance page.
- "€X more than expected" on yearly, quarterly and weekly ledger cards no longer invents overcharges.
- "Looks recurring" calls a weekly charge weekly with its real total, and no longer lists deposits into your own accounts.
- A category or merchant with "%" in its name opens instead of failing.
- With two notes carrying the same ledger tag, Finance always uses the same one (the one in `02-Areas/finance/` first) and says so, instead of alternating between them on every save.

Security:
- A finance ledger is only ever a note you can edit, and never one in another user's Personal folder: new rows can no longer land in a note another account keeps.

Internal:
- Statement files are read asynchronously and parsed once per version of the file (memory cache keyed by inode, size and mtime); category matching folds each rules set once. /api/finance and /api/home no longer block the event loop for hundreds of milliseconds.

1. "The Finance area", after the first paragraph ("…statements and payslips are real files in `02-Areas/finance/files/`."), add:

   > **Which note is a ledger.** Every finance singleton (`#subscriptions`, `#income`, `#payslips`, `#spending-categories`, `#own-accounts`, and the `#finance` area note) goes through `ledgerNote` in `routes/finance.ts`: only notes the reader can edit, never one in another user's `Personal/` folder, ranked the finance area first (`02-Areas/finance.md`, `02-Areas/finance/…`), then the reader's own places (`01-Me/`, `Personal/<me>/`), then the note made first, then path. "The first readable tagged note" was index order, and a save moves its note to the end of it, so two tagged notes alternated on every write (ACL-011). Two candidates for one ledger tag are reported (`ledgerConflicts`) and the Finance page says which one is used.
   >
   > **A ledger row is read or reported, never guessed.** `parseLedger` returns the rows it could not read (`unreadable`: line, field, cell): an amount that is not ONE number (`parseMoney` takes one number per cell, drops parenthesised asides, reads a lone separator before three digits as grouping and every minus sign), or a cadence it does not know. Cadences are `CADENCES` (a whole number of weeks or months each; an empty cell is monthly, "bimonthly" is deliberately not read).

2. "The Finance area", the paragraph starting "`loadStatements` (reached through `statementsFor`…": append:

   > Loading is asynchronous and cached in memory: each statement file's parse is kept while its inode, size and mtime are unchanged, and the merged result while the files and the own-accounts list are (FIN-015). Statements are `*.csv` (comma, `;` or tab, read off the header) and `*.xlsx` (first sheet, via exceljs); a file that gives no rows, or is in another currency, is listed in `spending.excluded` with the reason, and the upload answers with the same summary.

3. "The Planning tab", replace "`charge` and `income` (the ledgers, expanded across the range with `advance`, because `next` is one date and a month view spans six weeks)" with:

   > `charge` and `income` (the ledgers, expanded across the range with `chargeDates` from the note's own date — `anchor`, not the rolled-forward `next` — so a 31st stays the 31st and a range before `next` still shows its charges; the Finance ledger calendar uses the same function)
