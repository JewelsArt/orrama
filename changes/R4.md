# R4: The Orrery

The map leaves Notes and becomes its own place, the Orrery (rail 4; under More on the phone):
Orr's head in the middle, **Kind** for the whole vault and **Steps** for one thing in the
middle, take B ("a brain in each slice") as Jules picked it on 9 October. Ported from the
prototype (`~/orrama-redesign/orrery/orrery.js`), typed, and fed by the app's own data. Branch
`design/R4-orrery`, 6 commits on `7d1f5ed`.

## Built

- The model: the vault as the Orrery draws it, from four GETs the app already serves (no new endpoint); kinds, life areas, topics, one edge per pair, relations in both directions in the boards' words (0cdff44)
- The two layouts (Kind: rings by kind, slices by count^0.8; Steps: ring 1 by relation, ring 2 by what ring 1 shares) and the free web held in its slice and ring, settled off screen in slices (0cdff44)
- The canvas engine: renderer, live web, ripple, opening, Steps and back, area and topic zoom, drag/pan/wheel/pinch, arrow keys; a frame never resizes the canvas, reads CSS or layout, and strokes links in two passes (4ebdc27)
- The page at `/orrery`: header with Kind/Steps and Find, the map box with its path, legend and zoom, the panel (whole vault, an area, a thing with its relations), addresses for every level, reading/empty/error states, live sync, the phone layout (71a44aa)
- The map removed from Notes, a link to the Orrery in its place; GraphCanvas, its settings panel and the physics sliders deleted (db31ed6)
- Find's results kept inside the window (de68480)
- The Orrery loads lazily, when you first go there (the last commit, with these notes)

**Frame time at 10,000 files** (WebKit/WKWebView, the desktop app's engine, 1440×900, the R4
scratch server on a fictional vault of 10,003 files and 17,078 links, three runs): dragging a
file costs **3.0 ms a frame on average, p95 4–5 ms**, at 60 fps (simulating ~1,200 files in its
neighbourhood each frame); a full redraw 1.3 ms; one physics step over the whole vault 8.3–8.6 ms;
from page load to the whole vault laid out, landed and still: 6.6 s (the read is 0.6 s, the
settle ~0.8 s, the rest the opening and the web settling after it lands). The prototype's
figure was ~9 ms a frame. Measured with a scratch-only build that put the engine on `window`
(not committed); the probe is a Swift WKWebView script in the session's scratchpad.

## Board vs app: differences left on purpose

- **Kind / Steps** is the system's `Segmented` look (text tabs, Orr's orange underline), not the prototype's boxed segment: one switch pattern in the app.
- **Steps' address is `?center=<slug>`** (structure.md §1.2 and R0's router), not `/orrery/<thing>` as the R4 prompt words it. The slug is a project's board slug, else the file name as a slug.
- **Topics are the vault's own**: folders below the top folder, and projects' notes. On the scratch vault, conversations whose `about` names an ordinary note have no relation (the ontology's `about` range is project, area, company, person), so they gather in their folder's topic, "Conversations". On the live vault that will be "Claude Sessions" in Claude & tools.
- **Kinds follow the app's pages**, not the ontology's types: projects from the Projects board, people and companies by `#contact`/`#company` (what People lists). So the live vault, which has no ontology note yet, still gets its rings; typed relations appear once the ontology note exists.
- **Loading**: the app reads the vault in one request, so while it reads there is no count, just "Reading your notes" and an orange bar that moves; when the read took over 250 ms the files then come out of Orr with "Reading your notes · n of N" (the count of files placed).
- **Error** doesn't name the folder (no endpoint gives its path yet; R1's Settings → Notes). "Choose another folder" and the empty state's "Point me at your notes folder" open Settings → Notes.
- **Ask Orr** and "Ask Orr about Work" open Orr's panel without a question typed in (R12 owns the panel).
- **Esc out of Steps keeps the thing selected** (the prototype dropped the selection); Orr's head and the path's "You" behave the same.
- **Descriptions**: the panel shows the note's own description; with none it builds one from real figures ("Kade Social report · 3 of 8 steps done · 12 relations", "edited 40 days ago").
- **Notes' empty pane** shows the EmptyState with "Open the Orrery" at its top left; R7 redraws Notes.
- **Pinch** on a touch screen was added (the spec says "scroll or pinch"); it is tested with synthetic pointers, not yet on a real phone.
