# R0: Foundation

The base the other sessions build on: Orrama's tokens and type, the design system in
`apps/web/src/ui`, Orr, the shell (rail, phone tabs, More), addresses, keys, the overlay stack,
the theme, one date format, and `app.css` split by area. Code names stay Basalt; the screen says
Orrama. Branch `design/R0-foundation`, 19 commits on `53f09aa`.

## Changelog lines (user-facing, for [Unreleased] → Changed / Added / Fixed / Removed)

Changed
- The app is called Orrama on screen: window title, sign-in, lock, first run, home-screen icon.
- New look: warm paper by day, night blue after dark, with Figtree type served by the app itself.
- The rail holds the eight places (Today, Planning, Notes, Orrery, Projects, People, Mail, Money) with Find, Ask Orr and your Orr at the foot. Reload, Dark and Back left it: use ⌘R, Settings → Appearance and the browser's Back.
- On a phone: a top bar and five tabs (Today, Planning, Ask Orr, Notes, More). More holds the other places and Settings.
- Number keys follow the rail: 1 Today, 2 Planning, 3 Notes, 4 Orrery, 5 Projects, 6 People, 7 Mail, 8 Money. Press ? to see every key.
- Your avatar is one of eight Orrs. If you had picked a rock face, you see its Orr until you pick again.
- Dates read "Fri 9 Oct" and times use the 24-hour clock everywhere.
- New note asks for its path in a dialog, and says under the field what was wrong.

Added
- Every place and thing has an address: reload keeps your place, Back and Forward work, and links can be shared.

Fixed
- ⌘← and ⌘[ no longer pull you out of a note, or throw away a mail draft, while you type.
- One broken page no longer blanks the whole window, and ⌘R still reloads.
- Starting or reloading in the light theme no longer flashes dark first.
- ⌘K opened from Settings appears on top; Escape closes the top overlay and only that one; Find and capture are proper dialogs.
- After the server restarts, open pages catch up by themselves; reconnects back off; a session ended elsewhere stops retrying.
- The app no longer goes blank when the browser refuses storage.

Removed
- Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono (Google Fonts).
- Glyph icons (✕ × ‹ › ✎ ↵) and keycaps on screen.

## Board vs app: differences left on purpose

- Older screens keep their own layouts on the new tokens until their wave. On a phone their own heading repeats the top bar's title ("Today", then "Dashboard").
- The phone top bar's title is the place's name. On the Today board it is the date, with a + (New) beside Find; both come with Today (R5) and the create dialogs.
- Settings is still the old modal over the page (R1); your Orr opens it at `/settings/you`, and in the stack it is a sheet, so what it opens goes over it.
- Ask Orr is the old panel, in the stack as a panel; opening it leaves focus where it was (R12 draws the system's).
- The Orrery's address shows the old map (R4).
- Sub-addresses are parsed and written but read by each screen in its wave: the project screen keeps its remembered tab whatever the address says (R8), Planning's day and date (R6).
- The More sheet and the update note on the phone are not drawn on any board: More is the system's Sheet with your Orr on top, then one row per place and Settings; a newer build puts a dot on More and a wide note inside it.
- A place's key is in its rail tooltip ("Today  1"); the boards draw no tooltips.
- The rail's Mail item has no unread dot: the shell has no count to show (R2 can provide one).
