## Board vs app: differences left on purpose
- "Typical by day 8: €964" (the pace marker) and "+€20 vs €64 by now" per category: no endpoint knows a typical month *by a given day*. The pace bar's marker shows where in the month today is ("day 11 of 31"); the sentence keeps the existing projection ("On pace for €2,627.00 by the end of October…"). A running month's categories say "typical €X"; a finished month says "+€29.99 vs €812.00" (the old top cards' figure, `typicalAround`).
- The board's sentence "Rent is in; everything else comes to €213.40" and "the range of your last twelve Octobers' first weeks" are not computed anywhere; not shown.
- Saving: the board's "Income − Commitments − Typical spending = €235" is not the model. The box lays out the real arithmetic (average in, average out, left over, headroom, target), the same lines as the Savings page.
- Shop names ("Albert Heijn" for `ALBERT HEIJN 1427`) need a mapping note: Jules chose "later". The slot is ready (the page shows the bank's name); the board's grey note under the shops is not drawn until the note exists.
- The drill-down's refund line "netted against 12 Sep" and "€16.70 after the refund": the server sends the purchase already net of its refund and does not link the two. The refund row says "refund, already off what it refunds".
- On the phone the overview keeps a small row with the month arrows and the upload button under the shell's top bar (the board puts upload in the top bar, which is the shell's).
- The category board's year has 102 purchases at €249 typical; the app shows its own data. The header says "a month on average" (the server's `stats.average`, which is what the dashed line is), not "typical".
- The Commitments strip shows charges, today and the past; it does not draw the dashed "maybe" day for a charge that looks recurring (the server gives its months, not its day).
