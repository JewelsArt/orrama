If the Licence Details name a pilot period, the Company uses Orrama **free of charge
for that period**, on every other term of this licence. At the end of the pilot, the
Company either signs Licence Details with a fee, or stops using Orrama. Nothing is

telling the other in writing.

## 6. Your data is yours

Your Data belongs to the Company and its Users. I claim no right to it. Orrama runs on
the Company's own computers or servers, and I don't receive Your Data.

I see Your Data only if the Company asks me to help and gives me access for that, and
only for as long as the help takes. If that access includes personal data, we sign a
data processing agreement first.

The Company can always leave: Orrama's export gives Your Data in open formats (notes
stay plain Markdown files), and ending this licence never deletes it.
