Orrama can’t read its key

Orrama keeps a key that unlocks the passwords it saved for you, like your mail and AI. The key is missing or can’t be read, so Orrama won’t start: a new key would lock those passwords away for good. Your notes are safe.

Orrama keeps its key in Windows Credential Manager, as com.orrama.desktop. Sign in as the person who set Orrama up on this computer, or put that entry back, then try again.
